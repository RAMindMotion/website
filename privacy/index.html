MindMotion Privacy Policy

1. About this policy

This policy explains how MindMotion Healthtech Private Limited (“MindMotion”, “we”, “us”) handles your personal data when you use our website (mindmotion.in) and the MindMotion app. Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the “DPDP Act”), we are the “Data Fiduciary” for this data.

  • Company: MindMotion Healthtech Private Limited, CIN U62090HR2026PTC143626
  • Registered office: WeWork DLF Forum, Cybercity, Phase II, Gurugram, Haryana 122002
  • Privacy contact: contact@mindmotion.in
  • Effective date: 18-Nov-2026

In short: we collect only what we need to give you personal health insights, we do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

2. What we collect

We collect what you give us, what you choose to connect, and basic technical data. Health information is sensitive, and we treat all of it with extra care.

On the website

  • Your email address, if you ask for early access
  • Basic technical data such as browser, device and pages viewed

In the app: what you give us

  • Account details: email, phone, name
  • Profile and health answers: age, sex, height, weight, training frequency, main activity, fitness level and health status
  • Goals: your long-term aim, your 12-week goal and optional weekly weight entries
  • Daily check-ins: your ratings for sleep quality, energy, soreness and stress
  • Meal photos you upload, and the meal details and nutrition estimates you confirm
  • Messages you send to the AI coach, and its replies

In the app: what you connect

Only after you connect a device or health platform and approve each data type, we read:

  • Resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep duration and stages, steps, active calories, VO2 max estimates, workouts and heart-rate zones
  • Sources: your wearable or health platform

In the app: technical data

  • Device type, operating system, app version and crash or usage logs

3. Why we use your data, and our basis for doing so

We use your data to:

  • Create your account and run the app
  • Calculate your readiness, fitness and recovery scores and show your trends
  • Power the AI coach, including the daily explanation and weekly review
  • Recognise your meals and estimate nutrition
  • Track your goals and streaks
  • Keep the service safe, fix problems and improve quality, including reviewing AI responses.
  • Contact you about your account or early access
  • Meet legal obligations

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Consent. We process your personal data based on your consent under Section 6 of the DPDP Act. We ask for it before we collect your data, in plain language, including the AI processing described in section 4. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing contact@mindmotion.in. Some features will stop working if you withdraw consent. In limited cases the law allows processing without consent (for example, to comply with a legal requirement). We will only rely on that where it applies.

4. AI processing

The AI coach and meal-photo recognition use AI models run by third-party providers. Today these are Google (Gemini) and Anthropic (Claude). We may add or change providers, and we will update this policy when we do.

  • What we send: your goal, profile details such as age and sex, summaries of your wearable data, check-ins and nutrition logs, recent coach messages, the meal photos you upload, and the question you ask.
  • What we do not send: your name, email address or phone number.
  • Please note: what you type or photograph is sent as you provide it. Avoid including details you do not want shared, such as names or contact details in a chat message or photo.
  • How providers may use it: providers process your data only to return a response to us.
  • Where: AI providers may process data outside India (see section 5).
  • Not medical advice: the AI coach does not diagnose conditions or recommend medication. Talk to a qualified healthcare professional about any health concern.

5. Who we share data with, and where it is stored

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run MindMotion and for that purpose. Our providers may process this data in data centres located outside India, including the United States. We do not choose a specific processing region for these services. Our application data is stored with Supabase on AWS infrastructure in Tokyo, Japan. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose data if the law requires it, or to a successor if MindMotion is sold or merged, in which case this policy continues to apply to your data.

6. Wearable and health-platform data

  • Your choice. You connect your wearable or health platform yourself, such as Apple Health or Health Connect, and we read only the data types you approve. You can revoke access at any time in your phone’s health settings or in your wearable provider’s account.
  • Wearable provider sign-in. If a provider asks you to sign in to connect, we do not store your password. We keep an access token so we can sync your data.
  • Apple Health and Health Connect. We use this data only to provide MindMotion’s features to you. We do not use it for advertising, marketing or data mining, and we do not sell it. We share it only with the service providers in section 5, and with AI providers only with your consent (section 3).
  • If you revoke access. We stop syncing. Data we already received stays in your account until you ask us to delete it.

7. How long we keep your data, and deleting it

We keep your data while your account is active and while we need it for the purposes in section 3.

  • Deleting your account. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time. For now, email contact@mindmotion.in and we will delete it within 30 days.
  • Inactive accounts. We review accounts with no activity for 12 months, and delete them or ask whether you want to keep them.
  • Legal requirements. We may keep limited data for as long as the law requires.

8. Security and data breaches

We protect your data with reasonable safeguards: encryption in transit and at rest, and access limited to people who need it. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act and Rules require.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Get a summary of the personal data we process and who we have shared it with
  • Correct, complete or update your data
  • Ask us to erase your data
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Have your complaint heard through our grievance process
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights for you if you die or cannot act

To use any of these rights, email contact@mindmotion.in. We will reply within 15 days, and in any case within the period the law sets. If you are not satisfied with our response to a complaint, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Children

MindMotion is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

11. Grievance officer and contact

For questions, requests or complaints about your data, contact our Grievance Officer:

  • Name or title: COO
  • Email: contact@mindmotion.in
  • Address: WeWork DLF Forum, Cybercity, Phase II, Gurugram, Haryana 122002

We will acknowledge your message and respond within 15 days.

12. Changes to this policy, and a health disclaimer

Changes. We may update this policy. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app or by email before it applies. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Health disclaimer. MindMotion offers general health education and lifestyle guidance. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any disease. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always talk to a qualified healthcare professional about your health.
